SILVER README FILE

In this version there are three video filter modes that can be activated with the F1-F3 keys, here is an explanation of those:
F1	pixel perfect: The game will be displayed to match its original art 1:1.
F2 	upscaled: Smoothly upscaled image to fill the viewport, this is the default mode.
F3	smart upscaled: Edges are cleaned and enhanced as the game fills the viewport.


Controls
Selecting a Hero
    Click on a hero with the left mouse button.
    Press the keys 1-3.
    Hold down the left mouse button and drag a green selection box around the hero.

Selecting multiple heroes
To select several heroes at once, you can either:
    Hold down the <ALT> key and click with the left mouse button on each hero.
    Hold down the left mouse button and drag the green selection box around the heroes.

To select all heroes, press the <"> key.
Pressing the <Tab> key cycles through the selected characters making each in turn the primary hero.

Moving around
To walk, move the mouse pointer to where you want to go and click the left mouse button once. To run, move the mouse pointer to where you 
want to be and double click the left mouse button. To exit a scene, use the mouse pointer to find the doorway icon and click with the 
left mouse button. A doorway icon showing a no-entry sign denotes that the exit cannot currently be used. It may become open when enemies 
are killed or certain events are triggered.

Combat
To attack enemies with the primary hero, equip them with a weapon and use the following moves:
	Quick slash/jab - Hold down <CTRL> and click with the left mouse button.
	Left swipe - Hold down <CTRL> and the left mouse button while moving the mouse to the left.
	Right swipe - Hold down <CTRL> and the left mouse button while moving the mouse to the right.
	Lunge - Hold down <CTRL> and the left mouse button while moving the mouse forward.
	Backslash - Hold down <CTRL> and the left mouse button while moving the mouse backward.
	Use Special move - Hold down <CTRL> and the left mouse button (The special must be equipped first).
	Dodge - Hold down <CTRL> and tap the right mouse button.
	Use shield - Hold down <CTRL> and hold down the right mouse button.
	Fire ranged weapon - Hold down <CTRL>, move the sword icon to the target you wish to hit and click the left mouse button.
	Find Target - <SHIFT> key, this acts the same way as the <CTRL> key, but creates a larger target area around the enemy. It is useful for attacking small, fast enemies.
